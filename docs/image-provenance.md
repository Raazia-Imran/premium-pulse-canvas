# Website image provenance

The grayscale service and industry photographs in `src/assets/` were extracted from the client supplied company profile. Their `*-color.webp` counterparts were made by AI assisted colorization of those photographs; the colors are reconstructed and are not verified as the original scene colors. The Training circle reuses the colorized `service-training-color.webp` photograph.

The site describes what each image depicts. It does not claim that a photograph shows a specific HosH project or employee. Replace an image with an approved original color photograph when one is available. The homepage industrial scene and inspection object graphic were already part of the project before this revision.
