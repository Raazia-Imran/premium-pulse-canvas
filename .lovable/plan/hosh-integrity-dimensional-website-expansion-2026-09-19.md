# HosH Integrity — Dimensional Website Expansion

## Direction
Keep the approved bright industrial palette and typography, but replace flat grids with tactile spatial compositions: layered equipment imagery, depth shadows, responsive tilt, swipeable tracks, crystalline green glass, and restrained motion. Reference screenshots guide composition only and will not be embedded.

## Navigation and motion
- Add a crystal-green glass active state to the current page in desktop and mobile navigation.
- Add polished dropdown/icon/menu transitions, page-entry motion, shimmer accents, card resize behavior, and pointer tilt with glare.
- Disable tilt and intensive movement below 768px and for reduced-motion users; mobile cards remain swipeable with snap points.
